## `01` // Who We Are

Most of us don't really understand a piece of tech until we've broken it twice and fixed it at 2 AM.

**NITM-SOSC** is an open, student-run space at NIT Manipur for anyone who wants to go past lecture slides and lab assignments. We aren't tied to one programming language, tech stack, or branch — whether you write C, train models, tinker with Linux, or just opened your first GitHub account yesterday, you belong here.

No gatekeeping. Just curiosity, good code, and people willing to build.

## `04` // Jump In

You don't need to be an expert to contribute. Fixing a typo in a README, opening an issue, or asking a sharp question in Discussions all count.

1. **Say hello** in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/NITM-SOSC/discussions) and tell us what you're learning or building.
2. **Browse our [Repositories](https://github.com/orgs/NITM-SOSC/repositories)** and look for `good first issue` labels.
3. **Share a resource** — found a great article, tool, or roadmap? Drop it for others.
4. **Pitch a project** and team up with fellow students to build and maintain it in public.

> [!TIP]
> **New to Git & GitHub?** Start with our [Contribution Guidelines](https://github.com/NITM-SOSC/community/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) — we walk you through making your very first Pull Request step by step.

## `05` // Where We're Headed

We're starting right here at **NIT Manipur** to build an open-source culture that actually lasts — one that keeps growing long after any single batch graduates.

Over time, we want to bridge student developers across colleges and universities throughout **Manipur**, creating a stronger local builder ecosystem that contributes confidently to the global tech community.

We don't just want to be consumers of great software. We want to be the people who write it.
